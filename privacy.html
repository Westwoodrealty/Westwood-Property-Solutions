Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Premium Realty respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use this website and how that information may be used.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide through forms on our website.

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and telephone number
  • Property preferences
  • Information included in rental applications
  • Messages or enquiries submitted through our forms

2. How Information May Be Used

Information submitted through this website may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective clients, process property requests, and provide requested real-estate services.

3. Property Enquiries

If you contact us regarding a property, the information you provide may be used to communicate with you about that enquiry and related services.

4. Rental Applications

Information submitted through a rental application may be reviewed for the purpose of evaluating the application and communicating with the applicant.

Applicants should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through website forms unless specifically requested through an appropriate and secure process.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve functionality, understand website usage, or provide a better visitor experience.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services such as mapping, hosting, analytics, communication, or other technology providers. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Information Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain business records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Privacy Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information submitted through the website is handled, please contact Premium Realty through the contact information provided on our Contact page.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website, services, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be posted on this page.
